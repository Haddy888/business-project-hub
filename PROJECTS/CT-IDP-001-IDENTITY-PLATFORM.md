# Chosen Technology Identity Platform — Portfolio Index Entry

**Project ID:** CT-IDP-001  
**Parent:** Chosen Technology  
**Status:** Proposed program; implementation and launch not yet authorized  
**Working title:** Identity Platform

## Scope

A proposed Chosen Technology product division for identity verification, KYC, KYB, AML screening, fraud/risk workflows, enterprise APIs and a future reusable digital identity/credential ecosystem.

## Portfolio integration

- Chosen Technology: corporate product ownership and commercial operations.
- Brain Host / AEGIS: governed agent orchestration and work orders; existing canonical runtime must be verified and extended, not duplicated.
- CTEP / Sentinel: evidence verification, security review and authenticated human authorization.
- Digital Sales AI Protocol: controlled sales research, qualification, draft generation and human-approved external outreach.
